`artifacts/v6/`. Frontend measurements (framing, GPU benchmark, screenshots) are reported separately by the frontend agent
(`artifacts/v6/framing.json`, `bench_gpu_*.json`). The facility is a fictional, engineering-consistent digital twin (quasi-steady pandapipes
hydraulics + CompOpt chip thermals on the 4 instrumented racks and a lumped per-rack thermal model on the other 36); no claim is made that
it is a construction-certified piping design.

## V5 coupling (MuJoCo cell in the facility), `tests/test_v6_workcell.py`

End effector, payload and rover agree in the facility frame. Crawlers are refused arm commands; crawler cleaning targets its own segment and reports the plant result. Also covered: MuJoCo cell state is in the snapshot and a restore mid-grasp continues identically; an autonomous mission (`replace_strainer` on `strainer_01`) performs the pick/carry/seat sequence itself and the plant only changes after the mechanical gate (fail-closed negative included). An autonomous `replace_strainer` on `strainer_02` runs the same mechanical sequence and the plant changes only after the gate (`test_autonomous_exchange_on_the_second_strainer`). A new service cycle on a fixture that still holds the previous cartridge starts with a restock (logistics swap the used part for a fresh spare; counted in `spares_used`, emitted as `robot.workcell restocked`; `test_repeated_faults_get_repeated_mechanical_exchanges...`). ## Not done / not claimed

* No second engine worker: forks are verified, persisted, paused runs (not stepped); all non-active runs are read-only.
* `run_live` (the first recorded run) has no recoverable topology (config drifted before the registry existed): replay is refused honestly.
* INPIPE distribution is a decision, not a gap (DECISIONS 78): 1 crawler per supply header (5), 2 on return headers, 1 trunk, 1 FWS, 1 reserve, not 2 per row. Two per row would use all ten on the row headers and leave the FWS loop (a separate closed circuit, so no crawler can reach it from the TCS network) and the trunk without a crawler. Only the instrumented hero racks (A1, A2, B1, B2) can be fault targets.
* Valve authority on the lumped racks is small (opening 0.2 → −2 % flow); pump head / CDU UA were not re-tuned for 40 racks (no-fault run: margin 14–16 K, stable over 12 000 s).
* Traffic: lane + guard + yield is heuristic, not a proven multi-agent planner; under saturated random schedules robots hold and back off often (hundreds of hold ticks per 450 s).
* MuJoCo arm/gripper physics (V5) were not re-verified on the new layout; only the base-pose overwrite was removed.
* Neon retention (DECISIONS 76-77): stopped runs' interval snapshots are thinned at engine start (every 10th kept; first/last/incident/shutdown kept; the active run keeps its newest 150 dense), old LangGraph checkpoints keep the newest 20 per thread, and stream-only events (`world.diff`, `robot.pose`, `engine.health`, `heartbeat`) are persisted once per sim minute instead of every tick. `world_events` is append-only (DB trigger), so already-stored events cannot be removed: 326 MB of the 738 MB are that ledger. Before: 894 MB, the snapshot table was the largest. Backups of what was thinned: `~/lk-backups/` (outside the repo). `LOOPKEEPER_RETENTION=off` disables it.
* Performance (artifacts/v6/bench_gpu_*.json, `scripts/bench/run_bench.py --gpu`, production build, embedded simulator, headless Chromium on the RTX 3090 through ANGLE/Vulkan, drawing buffer verified equal to the viewport, GPU timer queries on): 3840x2160 p95 frame interval 2.4-5.5 ms in 9 of 10 scenario/preset runs (GPU p95 0.7-2.2 ms); the high-preset agent graph is the worst (189 fps, p95 15.1 ms, CPU-bound: 13.7 ms of JS per frame, GPU only 0.7 ms; cause not profiled). 1440x900: p95 2.1-7.8 ms. These are produced frames in a headless page without a display or vsync, so the harness itself refuses a 120 fps claim (`can_claim_120fps: false`). Still not measured: frames shown on a physical monitor; run `?bench=1` on that machine (the in-app Bench panel produces the same JSON).
